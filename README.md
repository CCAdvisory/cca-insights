# cca-insights

A curated repository of financial analysis, business case studies, valuation work, and educational finance content by Chopra Capital Advisory.

This repo is designed to showcase structured thinking across company analysis, startup finance, business strategy, financial modelling, and decision-focused research. The aim is to make finance more practical, readable, and useful for founders, SMEs, students, and decision-makers.

---

## Overview

CCAdvisory Insights is a research and content repository that brings together:

- Company analysis and valuation projects
- Business and startup case studies
- Finance explainers and educational notes
- Market and sector observations
- Financial modelling-based insights
- Decision-oriented business research

The focus is not only on reporting numbers, but on interpreting what those numbers mean for strategy, risk, capital allocation, and business performance.

---

## What You’ll Find Here

### 1. Company Analysis
Structured financial reviews of listed companies, including:
- historical financial statement analysis
- ratio analysis
- profitability and cash flow review
- valuation frameworks such as DCF and comparable company analysis
- key business and investment observations

### 2. Case Studies
Short and practical studies on startups, business failures, capital decisions, growth trade-offs, and operating strategy.

### 3. Finance Explainers
Educational content that simplifies concepts such as:
- three-statement modelling
- discounted cash flow valuation
- comparable company analysis
- working capital
- unit economics
- runway, burn, and financial clarity

### 4. Market Notes
Brief observations on sectors, business models, trends, and themes that matter to founders, operators, and analysts.

### 5. Templates and Frameworks
Reusable structures for analysis, presentation, financial thinking, and research writing.
