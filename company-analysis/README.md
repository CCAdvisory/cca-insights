# Company Analysis

This folder contains structured company analysis projects focused on understanding business performance, financial strength, operating trends, and valuation.

The work here is intended to combine financial statement analysis with business interpretation, so that numbers are not viewed in isolation but in the context of strategy, efficiency, risk, and long-term value creation.

---

## Objective

The purpose of this section is to study companies through a practical finance and business lens.

Each project typically aims to evaluate:
- business model and operating context
- historical financial performance
- profitability and efficiency trends
- balance sheet strength
- cash flow quality
- valuation approach and market perspective
- key strategic observations

---

Projects in this section may include:

## Typical Analysis Covered

A standard company analysis in this folder may cover:

- Historical income statement analysis

- Cash flow statement analysis
- Ratio analysis
- Revenue and profitability trends
- Working capital observations
- Forecasting of key financial line items
- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation
- Comparable Company Analysis (Comps)
- Business and strategic interpretation of results

---

## Approach

The analysis in this folder is built around a simple principle:

**Financial data becomes more useful when it is interpreted in a structured way.**

That means the goal is not just to calculate ratios or valuation outputs, but to understand what they suggest about:
- business quality
- capital efficiency
- sustainability of earnings
- financial discipline
- strategic positioning
- potential upside and risk
